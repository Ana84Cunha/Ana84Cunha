# Hi, I'm Ana 👋

I'm a QA Engineer based in Portugal, with a background in psychology. I also teach testing fundamentals to people starting their careers in QA.

I enjoy exploring how a product behaves, investigating unexpected results and questioning assumptions. My experience includes security-sensitive products and working with developers, product managers and designers from requirements through to release.

## How I work

- Review requirements and designs to identify gaps, clarify expected behaviour and discuss risks.
- Create test plans and prioritise coverage according to product risks and business needs.
- Design test scenarios and write test cases in Gherkin and other structured formats.
- Use exploratory testing to investigate behaviour beyond predefined test cases.
- Perform functional, regression, API and system integration testing.
- Report defects with clear reproduction steps and evidence, and verify fixes.
- Automate UI tests with Playwright and test APIs with Postman.
- Include security-focused checks around authentication, access control and session behaviour.
